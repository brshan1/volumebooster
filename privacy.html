Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 16, 2026

At Volume Booster : Max Sound EQ, we value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we access, use, and protect your data when using our application and Google Drive integration features.

1. Google Drive Access

Our app requests access to the Google Drive scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.readonly in order to allow users to browse, select, and play their own audio and video files stored in Google Drive.

Access is limited strictly to user-selected files required for playback and audio enhancement features inside the app.

2. Local Processing & No Data Collection

All audio processing, equalization, and volume enhancement are performed locally on your device.

  • We do not upload your Google Drive files to our servers.
  • We do not store, collect, or share your files or personal information.
  • We do not sell user data to third parties.
  • Your Google Drive data is used only for real-time playback and audio enhancement within the app.

3. Google API Services User Data Policy

Volume Booster : Max Sound EQ's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy , including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Security

We use Google's secure OAuth 2.0 authentication process for account authorization. Your files remain securely stored within Google's infrastructure at all times.

5. Data Deletion & Revoking Access

You can revoke the app’s access to your Google account and Google Drive files at any time through your Google Account permissions settings.
